OpenBook

Every book becomes absorbable. 大書無畏 — the more classic, thick and intimidating a book, the higher OpenBook's priority: its concepts taken apart into a curiosity staircase that leads readers back to the original book.

Founding — initialized, first release in preparation

What it is

A book goes in; what comes out is not a summary PDF but the full Dawn treatment: (1) Distillation — the book's actual claims, structure and strongest ideas as labelled canon cards, the book's words kept separate from the teacher's commentary, contested claims labelled contested.

(2) Story form — the ideas retold through story, the idempotent carrier into human memory. (3) Play form — OpenTeacher steps and games: one concept + one action per step, spot-checked on the +1/+3/+7/+21 ladder, boss-gated where the book's hard parts live.

(4) The reminding layer — the book keeps arriving at the moment of relevance, until the standard is met: 知道 → 使用到 → 行到出嚟 (know it → use it → live it). Absorption is measured by use, never by pages finished.

Every book also ships with a podcast/audio edition, heard in the listener's own most local accent / native tongue — Hong Kong Cantonese is the flagship register.

The supplement layer: a reader's supplement — labelled 讀者補充, never merged into the author's voice — joins the canon beside the book, and the next reader absorbs book + supplements together.

Guardrails: the book's claims are taught AS the book's claims, with the evidence layer beside them; distillation teaches ideas in Dawn's own words — full-text reproduction is never the product. Founding shelf: The Secret (book № 1), Jung's Red Book (book № 2), 《窮查理寶典》 Poor Charlie's Almanack (added to the 大書無畏 list 2026-11-21).

End Goal

Every person's “books I wish I knew” shelf becomes a curriculum they actually absorb — know it → use it → live it — and OpenBook succeeds when it makes the original book more desired: readers who go on to read, use, and live the books. The game is the door; the book is the house.
